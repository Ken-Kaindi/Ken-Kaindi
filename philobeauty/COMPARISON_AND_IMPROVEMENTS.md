# Reens Restaurant vs PhiloBeauty

Reens Restaurant was treated as the functional and layout reference. PhiloBeauty was not copied: its information architecture and stronger management ideas were adapted to the cosmetics business, then expanded with a distinct brand system and improved security.

| Area | Reens Restaurant reference | Original PhiloBeauty | Improved PhiloBeauty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand identity | Restaurant logo and maroon/green food theme | Text wordmark and abstract colour swatches | Approved PhiloBeauty logo, burgundy/gold/ivory cosmetics theme and Kenyan beauty photography |
| Location | Machakos restaurant | Nairobi references | Consistent Machakos Town business details and +254 743 432746 |
| Homepage | Image-led hero, features, specials and testimonials | Minimal swatch hero and product grid | Kenyan lifestyle hero, trust indicators, photographic categories, best sellers, service journey and testimonials |
| Catalogue | Menu categories, cards, details and cart | Cosmetics search, categories and cart | Enhanced product cards, quick add, local images, stock assurance and mobile layout |
| Customer objective | Customer registration and ordering | Registration and account history | Registration, secure login, history, payment visibility and direct tracking links |
| Tracking | Public order and delivery progress | No public tracking page | Tracking/order code plus phone verification, progress steps, ETA and payment summary |
| Payments | Separate restaurant payment records | Payment method stored only on order | Dedicated payment table, status, transaction reference and admin module |
| Deliveries | Rider and ETA module | Order status only | Rider details, ETA, dispatch/delivery times and automatic order-status synchronisation |
| Customers | Separate customer register | Users table only | Customer management view with order counts and lifetime value |
| Reports | Operational reports | Dashboard totals only | Date-filtered sales, top products, order status and payment-method report with print mode |
| Administration | Large staff dashboard | Basic products/categories/orders/messages | Modern responsive dashboard plus products, categories, orders, customers, payments, deliveries, reports and messages |
| Responsive design | Bootstrap-supported mobile navigation | Basic breakpoints | Custom responsive header, off-canvas admin menu, mobile bottom navigation, fluid grids and scroll-safe tables |
| Media | Local food photography | Remote placeholders | Optimised local WebP Kenyan cosmetic photographs plus SVG fallbacks |
| Security | Mixed | PDO, password hashing and CSRF | Added login session regeneration, email validation, CSRF state changes, safe redirects, conditional stock updates and safer one-time setup |

## Unique design decisions

- The approved PhiloBeauty logo appears in public and administrator layouts.
- Only **P** and **B** are capitalised in the brand name: **PhiloBeauty**.
- The primary palette is deep burgundy, champagne gold, warm ivory and muted blush.
- The photography represents modern Kenyan beauty customers and relevant skin tones without copying restaurant assets.
- Cosmetics are organised by routine rather than adapting restaurant terminology.
- Business reports focus on products, stock, payments, customers and delivery performance.
